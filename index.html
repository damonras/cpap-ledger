Import SD card data

Everything is read on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Pick the files from the SD card and they are saved in this browser. Importing again only adds new nights.

  1. Put the SD card in a reader and open it in the Files app.
  2. Choose STR.edf for the nightly summaries and charts across all nights.
  3. Choose files from DATALOG (the .edf files ending in BRP, PLD, EVE or SAD) for the detailed night charts. Pick as many nights as you like.

No SD card handy? A backup .json of nightly summaries (such as cpap-nights.json) fills in the Report and Charts. Nights already imported from the SD card are kept unless the backup shows more use.
